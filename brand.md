# Brand: Ajo on Lightning

Direction: adire cloth meets a market ledger. Warm, trustworthy, unmistakably West African, no crypto neon.

- **Indigo** (adire dye): the anchor, used for hero panels and primary actions in light mode.
- **Marigold**: money moving. Paid segments, the pot, primary actions in dark mode.
- **Cream paper**: light background. **Deep indigo night**: dark background.
- **Green** means paid and verified, **red** means late or failed. Neither is decoration.
- Type: Fraunces for headlines and amounts, Inter for UI, JetBrains Mono for keys and hashes.
- Signature element: the circle dial. Every member is a place on the ring, and the ring fills as they pay.
- Voice: plain, direct, a little warm. Say "the organiser", "the pot", "who paid". No em dashes.
